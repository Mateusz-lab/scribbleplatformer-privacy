Privacy Policy for Scribble Platformer

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application Scribble Platformer developed by Codedzilla.

1. Information Collection and Use

Scribble Platformer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or device data.

The game is fully playable offline and does not require registration, login, account creation, or internet connectivity.

2. Data Storage and Gameplay Progress

All game data, including level progress, high scores, collected items, and settings, is stored solely locally on your device within the application's private sandboxed storage (user://). This data never leaves your device.

3. Device Permissions

The application requests only the following standard Android permission:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to deliver subtle tactile haptic feedback when the player hits enemies, performs dodge rolls, or takes damage. No data is gathered or shared through this permission.

The game does not request Internet access, Location, Camera, Contacts, Microphone, or Storage permissions.

4. Advertising and Analytics

Scribble Platformer contains no advertisements and utilizes no third-party tracking, profiling, or analytics SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy

The game does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is designed to provide safe, family-friendly offline entertainment.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
